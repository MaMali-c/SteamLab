# Attribution and source notes (مشخصات منابع)

- IAPWS-IF97: International Association for the Properties of Water and Steam,
  R7-97(2012), https://iapws.org/technical-guidance/release/IF97-Rev . The small
  numerical reference fixtures identify their published source tables.
- Python implementation: `iapws`, https://github.com/jjgomera/iapws . This dependency
  is GPL-licensed; consult its license and the licenses of other dependencies when
  deciding how to distribute a public derivative. SteamLab is not endorsed or certified by IAPWS.
- Interface: Streamlit, https://streamlit.io/ . Data display: pandas, https://pandas.pydata.org/ .
- The original dashboard concept and `SteamTableSI.pdf` were supplied by the user.
  The scanned extract does not establish the book's full bibliographic record or
  permission to redistribute the complete PDF publicly. The personal ZIP includes
  the supplied reference, but Git ignores it by default.
- The 19-row sample is a factual transcription with provenance, not a new steam
  table model. It has been visually checked by the assistant, not independently certified.
- No project-wide open-source license is selected in V1.0; choose a compatible
  license before public distribution.
